Before merge, add  to your own hosting account
        and confirm the claim's DNS target matches its recommendation.
        GitHub Pages, Vercel, Netlify and Cloudflare Pages steps.

        
After your PR merges: wait for DNS sync, return to your host's
        domain settings, and wait for its HTTPS certificate. Then test your address.
        DNS alone is not enough; certificate issuance can take a while.
        A browser certificate error in that window - on your new address, and on
        GitHub Pages on your old github.io one too, since it now
        redirects - is expected. See the
        troubleshooting list.
