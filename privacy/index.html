Privacy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

got-a.page is a free, community-run service. This page explains what data is collected on this website, what is never collected, and who processes what. It covers the got-a.page website (home, docs, claim form, badges and gallery) and the hosted instant pages we render.

Analytics on this website only

Some pages of the main website (currently home, docs, claim and badges) use Google Analytics 4 to understand how the site is used. Google Analytics sets cookies and collects usage data such as pages visited, approximate region, device and browser type. How Google handles that data is described in Google's partner-sites policy. Pages without the tag, including the gallery and this page, collect nothing through it.

Claimed pages carry no got-a.page analytics or ads. Instant pages we render include no got-a.page analytics, cookies or advertising. Point-mode pages are served entirely by the owner's own hosting - what they collect or show is governed by that owner's policies, not this one.

What a claim makes public

Claiming a page creates a pull request on the public got-a-page/claim repository. Your GitHub username and the contents of your page file (name, tagline, links and records) are public and may persist in the repository's history even if later edited or removed. Never put private data in your page file.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-ups on got-a.page itself - claiming works through your own GitHub account.
  • No got-a.page analytics, cookies or advertising in the instant pages we render.
  • For point-mode names we publish DNS records only; the site behind the name is the owner's own.

Infrastructure providers

Serving got-a.page involves a small set of providers, each covered by its own privacy policy: GitHub (this website, the claim repository and pull requests) and Cloudflare (DNS and the worker that renders instant pages). Point-mode pages are hosted and governed by their owners and the hosts they choose, under their own policies.

Advertising

We don't show ads today. The standing decision for any future advertising is the main website only, through Google AdSense - claimed pages stay ad-free. If ads are enabled on the main site, Google and its partners may use cookies to serve and measure ads, as described in Google's advertising policy. This page will be updated before any ads go live.

Contact

Privacy questions: open an issue on the claim repo. Abuse reports: abuse@got-a.page.
