A few ways to make it yours

These owners opted in to share their pages. Their titles and taglines below are their own; this is not a directory of every claim or an endorsement of linked sites.

A link people can remember

Abhishek's page keeps it small: a title, a short tagline and a GitHub link. An Instant page can be that simple when your work already lives elsewhere.

Room for the story behind your work

Wolfiku's page adds an About section describing self-hosted tools and projects. Use page.sections when links alone do not explain what you do.

Want to try the structure without copying anyone's page? See the illustrative example and its JSON, then use the guided claim form. The gallery checkbox is optional. Gallery updates are published separately, so a new opt-in may not appear immediately.
